Privacy Policy — Farewell to Smoking

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Farewell to Smoking ("the App") is developed by Emre Dağ ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Data you enter into the App

The App lets you log cigarettes you smoke and view statistics about that history. All of this data — your records, your settings, your language preference — is stored only on your device, using local storage. It is never uploaded to us, we do not operate any server that receives it, and we have no access to it. Deleting the App or using its "Reset All Data" option erases it permanently; we cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Advertising

The App shows banner ads served by Google AdMob to support free use of the App. Ads are requested as non-personalized — the App does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes. Google AdMob may still collect limited technical data (such as device type, general location from IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can review Google's ad-related data practices at https://www.google.com/policies/technologies/ads/.

What we do not do

  • We do not require account creation, sign-in, or any personal identifier to use the App.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
  • We do not sell your data — we do not have any user data to sell.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone below the minimum age at which local law permits engaging with tobacco-cessation content. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
